# Research notes: market-demand

- Upwork marketplace earnings data, 2025: AI Integration demand up 178% and AI Chatbot Development up 71% year over year (platform data, strong). Source: https://aimagazine.com/globenewswire/3232122
- Gartner survey of 3,566 customers (Feb-Mar 2026): 87% say a company using GenAI for service must offer a human agent (large survey, strong). Source: https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-08-04-gartner-survey-finds-87-percent-of-customers-say-companies-using-genai-for-customer-service-must-provide-access-to-a-human-agent0
