# Research notes: demo-storyboarder

## Findings used

- Verizon Media and Publicis Media survey of 5,616 US adults (2019): 69% watch video with sound off in public places, 25% even in private; captions raised mobile ad recall by 8%; 80% of caption users are not hearing impaired. Source: https://www.streamingmedia.com/Articles/News/Online-Video-News/80-of-Video-Caption-Users-Arent-Hearing-Impaired-Finds-Verizon-131860.aspx
- LinkedIn native video of 30-90 seconds averages about 5.6% engagement (vendor-compiled). Source: https://dataslayer.ai/blog/linkedin-algorithm-february-2026-whats-working-now

## Targets that shaped it

- Frontdesk LinkedIn video (Nov 2026): a first version with a 3D node scene and synthesized music was rejected as "ugly"; the accepted version used the real chat transcript, a few callouts and UI sounds only, 36 seconds at 1080x1350. The rules above come from that rewrite.
