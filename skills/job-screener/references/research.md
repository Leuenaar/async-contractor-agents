# Research notes: job-screener

## Findings used

- Upwork's own guide names client red flags: unverified payment or fake-looking reviews, requests for unpaid test work, pushing communication or payment off-platform, rates far below market, scope creep, unrealistic turnaround. Source: https://www.upwork.com/resources/client-red-flags
- Applying many times to the same company lowers the interview rate (6.07% for one application versus 1.91% for 8+), and 11-20 targeted applications interview at 9.25% versus 2.58% for 100+. Tailored applications interview at about twice the rate. Source: Huntr Q1 2026 data, https://api.huntr.co/research/job-search-trends-q1-2026
- Remote-only roles interview at a lower rate (3.63%) than onsite (5.76%), so remote listings need tighter fit, not more volume. Same source.
- 93% of job seekers in that survey suspect they applied to a ghost posting; freshness and a visible hiring process are worth checking.

## Targets that shaped it

- 9 Nov 2026 screening of about 70 listings across Indeed (ID, MY, SG), LinkedIn and Upwork: nearly every automation role failed on years of experience, required coding, or live discovery calls. One Upwork lead-automation job passed. The reject table mirrors those real failures.
- A Workflow Automation role on Arc was recommended, then found already applied and declined: the reason the tracker check is a required step.
