# Research notes: evidence-auditor

## Findings used

- FTC Final Rule on Consumer Reviews and Testimonials (in force 21 Oct 2024): bans testimonials that misrepresent experience; penalties up to $51,744 per violation. Source: https://www.morganlewis.com/pubs/2024/08/ftc-issues-final-rule-on-consumer-reviews-and-testimonials
- Superpowers' "verification before completion" skill: no completion claims without fresh verification evidence; words like "should", "probably", "seems to" are red flags; a claim needs the command output that proves it, not a previous run. This skill applies the same idea to claims about work. Source: https://github.com/obra/superpowers
- TopResume survey (May 2025): 19.6% of hiring managers would reject an AI-generated application, and detection takes about 20 seconds. Overstatement is easy to spot. Source: https://topresume.com/career-advice/ai-in-hiring-survey
