# Research notes: scope-writer

## Findings used

- PMI Pulse of the Profession (2018): 52% of projects completed in the prior 12 months had scope creep, up from 43% five years earlier. PMI's advice: define what is out of bounds as well as in scope, agree both early, and put a change procedure in the contract. Source: https://www.pmi.org/learning/library/scope-creep-rising-11308
- Upwork lists scope creep without new milestones as a client red flag. Source: https://www.upwork.com/resources/client-red-flags

## Targets that shaped it

- Upwork lead-automation job (Nov 2026): the client already wrote testable deliverables ("test valid submissions, invalid emails, duplicate leads, and category rules") and made AI categorisation an optional separate quote. That is the model this skill copies.
- A Singapore professional services firm's onboarding-automation brief (Arc, Oct 2026) asked contractors to "scope each piece of work in plain language and estimate effort upfront", direct evidence that buyers value this step.
