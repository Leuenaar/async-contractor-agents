# Research notes: tracker-keeper

## Findings used

- Median time to a first interview is 29 days and to a first offer 108 days (Huntr Q1 2026); the 90th percentile wait for a first interview is over four months. Applications reply long after the owner has forgotten them, which is the whole reason for this skill. Source: https://api.huntr.co/research/job-search-trends-q1-2026
- Interview rate falls from 6.07% to 1.91% when someone applies 8+ times to the same company (same source), so duplicate detection matters.

## Targets that shaped it

- 9 Nov 2026: an Arc listing was recommended, then the platform showed it had been applied to 13 days earlier and declined. A complete tracker would have caught it.
- micro1 dashboard (Nov 2026) held three applications with different statuses (did not qualify, not selected, interview pending) that were not in any tracker.
