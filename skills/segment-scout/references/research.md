# Research notes: segment-scout

## Findings used

- Common B2B scoring splits weight about 65% fit and 35% intent, with negative signals subtracted outside the total and tier thresholds (A 80+, B 60-79, C below 60). Qualified leads convert around 40% versus 11% unqualified; negative scoring in one case cut lead volume 40% and lifted win rate 22%. These are vendor-reported, directional numbers. Source: https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/b2b-icp-scoring-framework-2026-lead-qualification-playbook
- The weights here differ on purpose: a solo contractor with no reviews wins on proof, not on brand, so "strength of proof" gets 25% and intent signals are folded into "pain visibility".
- 8 Nov 2026 positioning research (8 parallel research passes) found agencies are the buyers most willing to work async and written-first, and trade businesses with 1-20 staff are the second segment. That research shaped the reachability and negative-signal rows.

## Targets that shaped it

- Singapore and Australia trade businesses (plumbing, aircon, pest control) contacted in Oct-Nov 2026: zero replies from 31 emails, partly due to deliverability. Lesson: reachability must be scored, not assumed.
- A Singapore AI-ops agency found on 9 Nov 2026 (one agent per job, human approves) matched the owner's design principles, an example of a segment worth scoring: small AI agencies needing white-label builds.
