# Research notes: break-log-writer

## Findings used (vendor-compiled, directional)

- LinkedIn 2026: document carousels average about 6.6% engagement, native video 30-90s about 5.6%, image plus text 3.2%, text-only 2.0%. Posts with external links get about 60% less reach, and moving the link to the first comment is also penalised. The first 150 characters show before "see more". Stacked hashtags no longer help; 1-2 relevant tags. Engagement-bait prompts are penalised. "One valuable post per week outperforms five forgettable ones." Source: https://dataslayer.ai/blog/linkedin-algorithm-february-2026-whats-working-now
- The source itself warns its figures come from third parties without full methodology; treat them as direction, then check the owner's own post analytics.
