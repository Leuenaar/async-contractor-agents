# Research notes: form-filler

## Findings used

- A survey of 600 US hiring managers (TopResume, May 2025): 19.6% would reject a candidate whose resume or cover letter is AI-generated, 40.8% say AI is unacceptable in skills assessments, and in a blind test 33.5% identified the AI-written resumes in about 20 seconds each. Source: https://topresume.com/career-advice/ai-in-hiring-survey
- 52% in the same survey accept AI for drafting support, which is why this skill drafts and the owner edits, and why answers must sound like the owner.
- Applications with specific numbers in the summary got interviews at 6.97% versus 4.78% without digits (Huntr Q1 2026, near the edge of significance). Source: https://api.huntr.co/research/job-search-trends-q1-2026

## Targets that shaped it

- Productive Playhouse "Indonesian AI Product Evaluator" form (Nov 2026): CEFR self-rating, voice-recording experience, verifiable translation years "checked by reference", referral name, prefilled address from an older application. Each became a rule above.
- Sigma AI and TSMG forms (Nov 2026): 140-character pitch and short comment boxes; the reason limits are counted.
