# Research notes: case-study-writer

## Findings used

- Blue Whale Research, 2025 Tech Buyer Preferences Survey: the challenge-and-solution description is the most valued case study element overall (31.82%), and 44-53% of buyers at companies under 50 employees prioritise it. Large enterprises lean to outcomes and benchmarks. Sample size not reported. Source: https://bluewhaleresearch.com/?p=14132
- The same survey stresses measurable results and real names and quotes for credibility, which for a solo contractor with demo projects means test evidence and honest demo labelling instead of borrowed logos.
- FTC rule on reviews and testimonials (in force 21 Oct 2024): testimonials may not misrepresent the reviewer's experience. A fictional client must not read as real. Source: https://www.morganlewis.com/pubs/2024/08/ftc-issues-final-rule-on-consumer-reviews-and-testimonials

## Targets that shaped it

- The FirstTouch and CollageDepot case studies on leuenaar.my.id (2026): both use a fictional client and test data. The first-paragraph demo rule comes from them.
- A 9 Nov 2026 audit found the portfolio pages are a client-rendered single-page app: crawlers and AI tools saw only meta tags. Case studies should also exist as plain text (doc, Contra project, README).
