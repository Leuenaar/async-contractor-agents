# Research notes: constraint-strategist

## Findings from the 8 Nov 2026 run that shaped the method

- Timezone: "brief in the evening, delivered by morning" is a standard pitch in Asian outsourcing. Sources: https://unity-connect.com/our-resources/bpo-learning-center/advantage-of-Time-zone-in-outsourcing/ and https://devico.io/blog/turn-the-offshore-time-zone-difference-in-your-favour
- No calls: Designjoy runs a productized design service with no meetings, Zoom or Slack, all through Trello, reported at about $1.3M a year. Source: https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/i-make-1m-yearly-solopreneur-productised-service-never-have-calls-clients-1728621
- No reviews: Upwork Project Catalog lets buyers check out a fixed scope and price without a job post or bidding. Source: https://investors.upwork.com/news-releases/news-release-details/upwork-launches-project-catalogtm-new-way-businesses-and
- White-label for agencies: the agency owns the client and the calls; 81% of agencies outsource some work (AgencyAnalytics); one partner program shows fixed prices and 40-50% partner margins. Sources: https://agencyanalytics.com/blog/marketing-agency-benchmarks-management and https://logicworkflow.pages.dev/partners
- The run's verdict: white-label for agencies first, productized fixed-scope packages in parallel. Both examples were labelled directional because neither is specific to automation buyers.
