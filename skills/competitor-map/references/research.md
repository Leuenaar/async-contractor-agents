# Research notes: competitor-map

## Findings from the 8 Nov 2026 run that shaped the method

- The crowd's headlines repeat: "I will build n8n automation / AI agent / workflow", "save 20+ hours/week". Proof shown: workflow canvas screenshots, project counts, ratings. No sampled seller showed what happens when their system breaks. Sources: https://www.fiverr.com/s/8zkZd5r and https://www.upwork.com/services/product/development-it-n8n-automation-workflow-save-20-hours-week-2065871659405640848
- Price bands: Fiverr gigs from $150 per workflow; Upwork average about $39/hour; Upwork automation job volume down 34% from its July peak (third-party analysis of 53,786 postings, which the page itself does not fully reconcile). Source: https://ascendautomationagency.github.io/upwork-analysis/upwork-analysis.html
- Thin areas with demand evidence: failure-tested builds, fixed-price audit and repair ($750 for 3-5 workflows, 39 orders on one gig), single-decision AI with guardrails, trade verticals. Source: https://fiverr.com/nociones/clean-up-and-fix-your-n8n-workflows-and-automations
- A white space for a solo seller needs a matching proof asset; for this owner that was the FirstTouch failure log and the Frontdesk pricing design.
