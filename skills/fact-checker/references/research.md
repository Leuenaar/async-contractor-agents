# Research notes: fact-checker

## Findings used

- Caulfield and Wineburg (American Educator, Spring 2025): lateral reading means leaving the page to see what others say about a claim and its source; SIFT is Stop, Investigate the source, Find other coverage, Trace to the original context. In one study, students showed a sixfold increase in fact-checking techniques after seven hours of instruction, and context-seeking of about 30 seconds improved reasoning. Source: https://www.aft.org/ae/spring2025/caulfield_wineburg

## Targets that shaped it

- The premortem "30% better at identifying reasons" claim: tracing it to Mitchell, Russo and Pennington (1989) showed the study measured the number of reasons, not accuracy. Step 4 exists because of this kind of drift. Source: https://corporate.jcx.au/premortem
