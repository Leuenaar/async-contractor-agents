# Research notes: outreach-drafter

## Findings used

- Instantly 2026 cold email benchmark: average reply rate 3.43%, top quartile 5.5%+, elite 10.7%+. First-touch emails under 80 words perform best. Step 1 drives 58% of replies and follow-ups 42%; bounce rate should stay under 2%. Source: https://instantly.ai/cold-email-benchmark-report-2026
- The owner chose not to send follow-ups. The 42% follow-up share is the price of that choice and is stated so it can be revisited with data.
- Problem-focused messaging and micro-segmentation are named drivers of top results (same source, no quantified lift).

## Targets that shaped it

- 16 Singapore emails in Oct 2026 contained google.com/url wrapped links and were voided; 15 Australia emails went out in 51 seconds. Both mistakes became rules (no wrappers, spacing between sends).
- The Agentplace email of 28 Oct 2026 set the layout: "Hi Name," short paragraphs, two bold-labelled lines, the proof link on its own line, signature with Website and LinkedIn.
- Zero replies across 31 trade-business emails, with a missing PTR record at the mail host: deliverability is checked before copy is blamed.
