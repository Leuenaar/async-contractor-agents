# Research notes: upwork-proposal

## Findings used (vendor data, directional)

- Reply rate by length (Getmany via SnipeWork): under 50 words 9.4%, 100-149 words 6.7% (weakest), 700+ words 18.5%. The middle band does worst; length should follow the post's detail.
- Timing (TrendsOnUp): under 5 minutes after posting 9%, 12-60 minutes 8-24% for fast agencies, 2-4 hours 1-3%.
- Closing with an invitation to questions lifted reply rate 5.89 points, the largest phrase effect reported.
- Video: under $500 projects text converts 8.7% versus video 1.2%; on $10K+ projects video 31.2% versus text 5.8%. A recorded walkthrough fits large, detailed posts only.
- 5-10 tailored proposals a week beat 30+ sprayed a day. First contract within 30 days links to 3x odds of sustainable income (UpHunt).
- Compiled source: https://jobhunt-docs.pages.dev/strategy/upwork/proposal-research-2026/
- Template openers ("I have experience with", "Hi! Thanks for posting") cut reply rates 3.7-5.5 points; one r/Upwork client estimated 80-90% of proposals received are AI-written. Source: https://gigradar.io/blog/ai-proposals-upwork

## Targets that shaped it

- Upwork "n8n Developer Needed for Website Lead Automation" (posted 7 Nov 2026, $300 fixed): requires the keyword LEADFLOW first, lists test cases (valid, invalid email, duplicate, category rules), asks for written setup notes and a short screen recording. This post is the model for steps 3 to 5.
- Script.it paid test job: required 5+ paid client projects; the reason for the "do not claim" rule.
