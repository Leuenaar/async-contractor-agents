# Research notes: handover-writer

## Findings used

- Google SRE workbook: each alert should have a matching playbook entry with severity, impact, debugging suggestions and actions; playbooks reduce stress, mean time to repair and human error; they go stale as fast as the system changes; a fixed sequence run on every alert should become automation. Source: https://sre.google/workbook/on-call/
- Automation agencies report failures clustering around day 30 when no internal owner exists; one operator claims requiring a named owner before building cut churn by about 60% (single anecdotal source). Source: https://bittide.aicompass.dev/article/b2614d1f-a2e2-4f8a-a650-849da034e1ae
- A Singapore professional services firm's job brief (Oct 2026) required "document each workflow so a non-technical team can understand and maintain it", direct buyer evidence.

## Targets that shaped it

- The Upwork lead-automation job (Nov 2026) asked for "short written setup and maintenance instructions" and "a short screen recording showing how to edit category rules and email templates". Steps 3 and 6 match.
