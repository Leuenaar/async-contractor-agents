# Research notes: humanizer

## Findings used

- Kobak et al. (2024-2025), excess vocabulary in 15 million PubMed abstracts: at least 13.5% of 2024 abstracts show LLM processing, found only from abrupt rises in style words. Word choice alone gives AI text away. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.07016
- TopResume survey of 600 US hiring managers (May 2025): 33.5% correctly picked out AI-written resumes in about 20 seconds each; 19.6% would reject an AI-generated resume or cover letter. Source: https://topresume.com/career-advice/ai-in-hiring-survey
- On Upwork, template openers cut reply rates by 3.7-5.5 points (GigRadar, vendor data). Source: https://gigradar.io/blog/ai-proposals-upwork

## Targets that shaped it

- The owner's own feedback in Oct-Nov 2026: drafts were called "too AI" even when professional; a Contra caption was rejected as "AI banget". The fact-preservation step exists because an early rewrite softened a real number.
